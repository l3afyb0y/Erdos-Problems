# Erdős Problem #155

**Database status at attempt:** OPEN (checked 2026-08-30)

**Final classification:** useful reformulation / exact tail-difference tax

## Statement

Let `F(N)` be the maximum size of a Sidon subset of `{1,...,N}`. Is it true that for every fixed `k>=1`,

\[
F(N+k)\le F(N)+1
\]

for all sufficiently large `N`?

Current status source: https://www.erdosproblems.com/155

## Exact necessary inequality for a Sidon set crossing the boundary

Let `A subset [1,N+k]` be Sidon. Put

\[
B=A\cap[1,N],\qquad T=A\cap[N+1,N+k],
\]

and write `b=|B|`, `t=|T|`.

For a Sidon set, all positive differences between distinct elements are distinct. In particular, the positive-difference sets `Delta(B)` and `Delta(T)` are internally collision-free. They are also disjoint: if

\[
b_2-b_1=t_2-t_1>0
\]

with `b_i in B` and `t_i in T`, then

\[
b_2+t_1=b_1+t_2,
\]

a nontrivial equality of two unordered sums from `A`, contradicting Sidonicity.

For fixed `k` and `N>k`, both difference sets lie in `{1,...,N-1}`. Hence

\[
\boxed{\binom b2+\binom t2\le N-1.}
\]

Equivalently, any attempt to gain `t` points in the last `k` positions consumes `binom(t,2)` difference slots that the first `N` positions are forbidden to use.

This yields the explicit universal bound

\[
b\le \left\lfloor\frac{1+\sqrt{1+8(N-1-\binom t2)}}2\right\rfloor,
\]

and hence a finite optimization over `0<=t<=F(k)` for `F(N+k)`.

## Why this does not solve #155

For fixed `k`, the missing term is only `O_k(1)` inside a pool of `N-1` possible positive differences. The inequality therefore controls the Euclidean-size envelope of a Sidon set but does not compare the constrained optimum in `[1,N]` with the *actual* extremal value `F(N)` sharply enough to force a loss of `t-1` points.

A stronger exact split inequality, `F(N+k)<=F(N)+F(k)`, has also been publicly kernel-checked in August 2026; it likewise leaves the `k>=2` gap unresolved.

## Prior-art / current-claim audit

- Erdős Problems #155 remains OPEN.
- A 2026 public formalization records the exact subadditive split `F(N+k)<=F(N)+F(k)` and explicitly leaves the stated eventual `+1` conjecture open: https://www.jig.so/p/57
- No complete proof or disproof was located in the narrow audit.

The difference-slot inequality above is recorded as a useful exact reformulation, not as a novelty claim and not as a solution.
