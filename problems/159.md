# Erdős Problem #159

**Database status at attempt:** OPEN (checked 2026-08-30)

**Final classification:** known/basic structural reduction / no polynomial progress

## Statement

Prove that there is a constant `c>0` such that

\[
R(C_4,K_n)\ll n^{2-c}.
\]

Current status source: https://www.erdosproblems.com/159

The maintained record gives the current window

\[
\frac{n^{3/2}}{(\log n)^{3/2}}\ll R(C_4,K_n)\ll \frac{n^2}{(\log n)^2}.
\]

## Exact local structure

Let `G` be `C_4`-free and let `v` be any vertex. Then the induced graph on `N(v)` has maximum degree at most one.

Indeed, if a neighbor `u` of `v` had two neighbors `x,y` inside `N(v)`, then

\[
v-x-u-y-v
\]

would be a 4-cycle. Therefore `G[N(v)]` is a matching together with isolated vertices.

Consequences:

1. every edge of `G` belongs to at most one triangle;
2. every neighborhood contains an independent set of size at least `ceil(d(v)/2)`;
3. hence

\[
\alpha(G)\ge \frac{\Delta(G)}2.
\]

Combining this with a general greedy/Caro-Wei bound gives only square-root-scale control after optimizing in the degree. The stronger logarithmic gain in the classical upper bound for `R(C_4,K_n)` comes from exploiting local sparsity much more efficiently.

## Discovery attempt and barrier

The language-first target was to turn “no two-step routes may share both endpoints twice” into a polynomial gain in the independence number. The matching-neighborhood structure is exact and unusually rigid, but by itself it is still compatible with degree about `sqrt(N)`, for which the elementary independent-set lower bounds remain of order `sqrt(N)` (up to logarithmic improvements from deeper locally sparse graph results).

No invariant was found that upgrades this to `N^{1/2+epsilon}` for any fixed `epsilon>0`, which is what the conjectured Ramsey improvement would require.

## Prior-art / current-claim audit

A current-status search found no credible complete proof or disproof. The database was last materially updated in March 2026 and still lists #159 as open. The local matching-neighborhood observation is standard-level structure and is not claimed as novel.

**Bottom line:** no progress beyond clarifying the exact local obstruction behind the known locally sparse-graph route.
