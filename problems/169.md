# Erdős Problem #169

**Database status at attempt:** OPEN (checked 2026-08-30)

**Final classification:** useful reformulation / multicolour van-der-Waerden lower envelope

## Statement

For `k>=3`, let `f(k)` be the supremum of `sum_{n in A} 1/n` over sets of positive integers containing no `k`-term arithmetic progression. Estimate `f(k)`. In particular, is `f(k)/log W(k) -> infinity`, where `W(k)` is the 2-colour van der Waerden number?

Current status source: https://www.erdosproblems.com/169

## Exact lower-envelope observation

Let `W_r(k)` denote the least `N` such that every `r`-colouring of `[N]` has a monochromatic `k`-term arithmetic progression. By definition there is an `r`-colouring of `[W_r(k)-1]` with no monochromatic `k`-AP. Each colour class is therefore itself `k`-AP-free.

The harmonic masses of the `r` colour classes sum to `H_{W_r(k)-1}`. Hence at least one colour class has harmonic mass at least the average, giving

`f(k) >= H_{W_r(k)-1}/r`

for every `r>=2`. Consequently

`f(k) >= sup_{r>=2} H_{W_r(k)-1}/r`.

For `r=2` this is the familiar trivial `~(1/2) log W(k)` bound. The original question would follow from any family `r=r(k)` for which

`log W_r(k) / (r log W_2(k)) -> infinity`.

More modestly, any uniform improvement of this ratio above `1/2` yields a corresponding improvement to the currently stated trivial constant.

## Audit

The deduction is elementary and complete, but no sufficiently strong lower bound for multicolour van der Waerden numbers was found that improves the known frontier for #169. ## Bottom line

This is a useful exact reformulation, not a solution. The difficulty has been shifted to quantitative growth of multicolour van der Waerden numbers.
