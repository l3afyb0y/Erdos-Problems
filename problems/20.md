# Erdős Problem 20

**Database status checked 2026-08-30:** OPEN.

**Experiment classification:** known reduction independently rediscovered / no new progress.

## Problem

Let `f(n,k)` be the least size forcing a `k`-sunflower in an `n`-uniform family. Is `f(n,k) < c_k^n` for fixed `k`?

## Attempt

Let `F` be an `n`-uniform family containing no `k`-sunflower. A maximal pairwise-disjoint subfamily has size at most `k-1`, since `k` disjoint members would already be a sunflower with empty core. Hence the union `X` of that maximal disjoint family has size at most `(k-1)n`, and maximality implies every member of `F` meets `X`.

Therefore some `x in X` belongs to at least `|F|/((k-1)n)` members. Removing `x` from all members of that subfamily preserves sunflower-freeness: any `k`-sunflower after deletion would become a `k`-sunflower after adding the common element `x` back. This yields the classical recurrence

`g(n,k) <= (k-1)n g(n-1,k)`

for the maximum sunflower-free family size `g`, recovering the factorial-scale Erdős-Rado bound.

The attempted strengthening was to replace the single popular point by a bounded-size popular trace and thereby remove the factor `n` at every induction step. The obstruction is that a maximal disjoint transversal controls only total incidence with `X`; it does not force a bounded trace with constant-proportion mass. Families can spread their intersections across `Theta(n)` points, and the resulting trace decomposition returns to the logarithmic losses in modern sunflower arguments rather than a constant base.

## Prior-art audit

The maintained page records the modern bound `(C k log n)^n` and still lists the constant-base conjecture as open. The recurrence above is classical and no improvement to the current frontier was obtained.
