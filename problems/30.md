# Erdős Problem #30

**Database status at attempt:** OPEN

**Experiment status:** no progress

## Statement

Let `h(N)` be the maximum size of a Sidon set in `{1,...,N}`. Is it true that for every `epsilon>0`,

`h(N)=sqrt(N)+O_epsilon(N^epsilon)`?

## Attempt

The difference-packing formulation was expanded and pushed through the standard small-/large-difference and shift-incidence viewpoints. These recover the familiar fact that positive differences of a Sidon set are unique and lead toward the classical `sqrt(N)+O(N^{1/4})` family of upper bounds, but no new cancellation or boundary inequality was found that lowers the `N^{1/4}` error to a subpolynomial scale.

The construction side was also checked against Singer-type examples. Those explain the sharp main term but do not address the required uniform subpolynomial upper error.

## Prior-art audit

The maintained Erdős Problems page still lists #30 as OPEN. A 2026 Lean formalization of the Singer construction and Sidon infrastructure explicitly records an upper bound of the shape `sqrt(N)+N^{1/4}+O(1)` and treats the full subpolynomial-error statement as open. Nothing in this run improves that frontier.
