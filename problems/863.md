# Erdős Problem 863

**Database status checked 2026-08-30:** OPEN.

**Experiment classification:** known reduction independently rediscovered.

## Problem

Let `r >= 2`. Let `A subset {1,...,N}` have maximal size subject to the condition that every integer has at most `r` representations as `a+b` with `a <= b` and `a,b in A`. Let `B subset {1,...,N}` have maximal size subject to the condition that every positive integer has at most `r` representations as `a-b` with `a,b in B`.

Assuming

`|A| ~ c_r sqrt(N)` and `|B| ~ c'_r sqrt(N)`, is `c'_r < c_r` for every `r >= 2`?

## Short mathematical reduction

A construction of Cilleruelo, Ruzsa, and Trujillo gives

`c_r >= (r + floor(r/2)) / sqrt(r + 2 floor(r/2))`.

The standard difference-set upper bound gives

`c'_r <= sqrt(r)`.

Put `m = floor(r/2)`. For `r >= 2`, we have `m >= 1`. Compare the two bounds by squaring:

`((r+m)^2)/(r+2m) > r`

if and only if

`(r+m)^2 > r(r+2m)`

if and only if

`r^2 + 2rm + m^2 > r^2 + 2rm`

if and only if

`m^2 > 0`.

Therefore

`(r+m)/sqrt(r+2m) > sqrt(r)`.

Hence

`c_r >= (r+m)/sqrt(r+2m) > sqrt(r) >= c'_r`, so

**`c'_r < c_r` for every `r >= 2`, provided the quoted published bounds apply exactly as stated.**

## Complete prose proof

Let the integer `m` mean half of `r`, rounded down. Since `r` is at least two, `m` is positive. The known sum-representation construction guarantees a constant at least `(r+m)` divided by the square root of `(r+2m)`. The known difference-representation estimate guarantees a constant at most the square root of `r`.

So the only question is whether the first guaranteed quantity is larger than the second. Both are positive, so square them. After multiplying away the positive denominator, every term on the two sides is identical except that the sum-set side contains one additional term, `m^2`. Since `m` is positive, that extra term is positive. The lower bound for the sum constant is therefore strictly larger than the upper bound for the difference constant. The desired strict inequality follows.
