# Erdős Problem 710

**Current status checked 2026-08-30:** OPEN.

**Experiment status:** useful reformulation / no complete solution.

## Statement

Let `f(n)` be minimal such that the interval `(n,n+f(n))` contains distinct integers `x_1,...,x_n` with `k | x_k` for every `1<=k<=n`. Determine an asymptotic formula for `f(n)`.

## Exact Hall formulation

Fix a candidate length `L` and write

`J={n+1,...,n+L}`.

For each `k in {1,...,n}`, let

`N(k)=J intersect kZ`,

the allowed positions for the integer assigned to `k`.

Then a valid choice of distinct `x_k` exists if and only if the bipartite graph with left vertices `1,...,n` and right vertices `J` has a matching saturating the left side. By Hall's theorem this is equivalent to

`| union_{k in S} N(k) | >= |S|`

for every `S subset {1,...,n}`.

Thus the problem is exactly about how densely the divisibility neighborhoods of the first `n` moduli can collide inside a short interval immediately after `n`.

## Forced-position obstruction

If `k>L`, then two distinct multiples of `k` differ by more than the interval length. Hence `N(k)` has size at most one. Therefore any collection of moduli larger than `L` whose unique available multiple is the same integer produces an immediate Hall obstruction.

Equivalently, if some `q in J` has two distinct divisors `k_1,k_2` satisfying

`L<k_1,k_2<=n`

and `q` is the unique multiple of each divisor inside `J`, then length `L` cannot work.

This obstruction is exact, but it only operates in the regime `L<n`. The known scale for `f(n)` is much larger than `n`, so this branch does not touch the asymptotic frontier.

## Dependency interpretation

The assignment problem is not primarily about finding a multiple for each `k`; every `k` has many multiples once `L` is large. The hard condition is injectivity. A set of moduli fails precisely when too many of their candidate-multiple sets collapse onto too few positions. Hall deficiency is therefore the natural invariant:

`def(S)=|S|-|union_{k in S}N(k)|`.

A proof of the asymptotic formula would have to identify the subsets `S` maximizing this deficiency at the relevant scale, or prove they obey a universal structural description.
