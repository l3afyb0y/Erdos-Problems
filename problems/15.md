# Erdős Problem #15

**Database status at attempt:** OPEN

## Problem

Does

\[
\sum_{n\ge1}(-1)^n\frac{n}{p_n}
\]

converge?

## Exact pairing identity

Pair consecutive terms. Writing `d_n=p_{n+1}-p_n`,

\[
\frac{2m}{p_{2m}}-\frac{2m-1}{p_{2m-1}}
=
\frac1{p_{2m}}-
\frac{(2m-1)d_{2m-1}}{p_{2m-1}p_{2m}}.
\]

Therefore the even partial sums satisfy

\[
S_{2M}
=
\sum_{m\le M}\frac1{p_{2m}}
-
\sum_{m\le M}
\frac{(2m-1)(p_{2m}-p_{2m-1})}{p_{2m-1}p_{2m}}.
\]

The first term diverges like one half of `log log M`; convergence is therefore equivalent to a matching asymptotic cancellation from the weighted odd-index prime gaps, with a finite limiting remainder.

This makes the difficulty explicit: the question is not controlled merely by `p_n~n log n`; it needs parity-sensitive information about prime gaps strong enough to control a conditionally cancelling logarithmic divergence.

## Prior art

The maintained problem page records that Tao proved convergence under a strong Hardy--Littlewood prime-tuples hypothesis. No unconditional argument is known. The exact paired identity above is elementary and is recorded only as a useful reformulation, not as a novelty claim.
