# Erdős Problem #100

**Database status at attempt:** OPEN (checked 2026-08-30 against the maintained Erdős Problems record and current database mirrors).

## Statement

Let \(A\subset \mathbb R^2\) be a set of \(n\) points such that all pairwise distances are at least \(1\), and any two distinct determined distances differ by at least \(1\). Must the diameter \(D(A)\) satisfy \(D(A)\gg n\)?

## Attempt

Write the distinct positive distances as
\[
1=d_1<d_2<\cdots<d_s\le D.
\]
The separation hypothesis gives \(d_j\ge j\), hence \(s\le D\). Therefore every lower bound for the ordinary distinct-distance problem transfers immediately to a diameter lower bound. In particular the Guth--Katz bound \(s\gg n/\log n\) recovers
\[
D\gg \frac{n}{\log n}.
\]

The discovery pass tried to gain an additional factor from the stronger metric separation, rather than merely the number of distance classes. Around a fixed point \(x\), points at a fixed distance lie on a circle. Since such a shell is in convex position, a shell of size \(m\) itself determines at least \(\lfloor m/2\rfloor\) distinct chord lengths; however these chord-length classes can be reused across many shells and many centers. Summing this local information therefore does not yield a super-Guth--Katz global lower bound without an additional anti-reuse invariant.

A second route through isosceles-triangle energy has the same obstruction: the separation condition bounds the number of distance *values* but does not bound the multiplicity with which a distance value occurs.

## Adversarial check

No step above yields \(D\gg n\). The implication \(s\le D\) is exact after the normalization \(d_1=1\), but combining it with the current distinct-distance theorem gives only the already recorded \(n/\log n\) scale. Local circular-shell arguments do not justify summing distinct chord counts because the same global distance value may recur in unrelated shells.

## Prior-art note

The maintained problem record already states that Guth--Katz implies \(D\gg n/\log n\). No stronger result was obtained here.

## Final classification

**no-progress / known distinct-distance barrier.**
